What goes here

The final document is governed by Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012 and must cover at least the following, with the real legal name, tax ID and address.

  • Who the data controller is and how to reach them.
  • What data is collected and the purpose of each item.
  • The legal basis for each use, and what is optional.
  • The rights of the data subject: to access, update, correct and delete their data, and to withdraw consent.
  • How to exercise those rights and the response deadlines.
  • How long each item is kept, and what is kept for accounting obligations.
  • Who data is shared with and why.
